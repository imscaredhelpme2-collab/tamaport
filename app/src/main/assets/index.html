  How to play

How to take care your Tamagotchi
  1. Care
    2. When your Tamagotchi calls you, care for them by using the keys to select a Care icon. Use the Check Meters to find out what your Tamagotchi wants.
  2. Feed
    3. Select "Meal" or "Snack" to feed your Tamagotchi. They'll stop eating when they get full. If they eat too many snacks, they'll get sick more easily, so be careful.
  3. Light
    4. When your Tamagotchi gets sleepy, select the Light icon to turn off the light. *It will turn on automatically in the morning.
  4. Play
    5. In TOY mode
    Play a game of Look This Way with your Tamagotchi.Press the A or B Button, and if your Tamagotchi ends up looking the same direction as the button you pressed, they win. If they win a best-of-five match, they will cheer up.

    In APP mode
    Play a game of Rock, Paper, Scissors with your Tamagotchi. Touch the screen to choose your throw! Your Tamagotchi wins if they choose the winning throw or the same throw as player. If they win a best-of-five match, they will cheer up.
  5. Medicine
    6. If a skull icon appears, it means your Tamagotchi is sick. Select the Injection icon to cure them with an injection. The skull icon disappears once they're cured. (They can only be cured twice! The third time, they will die.)
  6. Potty Duck
    7. Your Tamagotchi goes to the toilet every few hours.When this happens, select the Potty Duck icon to flush. If you don't flush, your Tamagotchi may get sick.
  7. Health Meter
    8. You can check the Tamagotchi's age, weight, discipline, hunger and happiness.Feeding or Playing with Tamagotchi will increase the heart level.
  8. Discipline
    9. When it looks like your Tamagotchi is getting spoiled, select the Training icon to scold them.
  9. Call Signal
    10. This appears when your Tamagotchi is calling you. Be sure to take care of them while it is displayed.
How to take care your Tamagotchi
  1. Sleeping/Waking
    2. Your Tamagotchi goes to sleep when night falls, and wakes up in the morning.
    *When they go to sleep and wake up depends on their maturity and whether they are good or bad.
    Young Tamagotchis go to sleep and wake up early, but as they get older they stay up later. Undisciplined Tamagotchis also stay up late.
    *Your Tamagotchi can't go to sleep until you turn out the light for them.
    *You can't take care of Tamagotchis while they are sleeping.
  2. Toilet
    3. Sometimes your Tamagotchi poops. When this happens, flush in Potty Duck mode.
  3. Call
    4. Tamagotchis call their owner when their status changes. When this happens, you should see to their needs. If you take good care of them, they will call you between four and nine times a day.
    ●If they are hungry, feed them.
    ●If they are in a bad mood, give them snacks or let them win a game.
    ●If they are sleepy, turn the light out.
    ●If they are spoiled (this happens when their hunger or mood is higher than 0), scold them using the Training icon.
  4. Sickness
    5. When your Tamagotchi gets sick, cure them in Medicine mode. *Tamagotchis get sick more often when they are young or undisciplined.
    Reasons for sickness:
    ●Natural causes
    ●Eating too many snacks
    ●Not flushing the toilet
  5. Share
    6. Select the Share button to upload Tamagotchis you've raised to Facebook, or save photos of them to the photo folder.
  6. Gallery
    7. View the Tamagotchis you've raised. You can check how many times you've raised a Tamagotchi on its details screen. Collect lots of Tamagotchis to build your wallpaper collection. Raise new Tamagotchis to build your shell collection.

  FAQ

About service
  1. Does "Tamagotchi Classic" support horizontal screen mode?
    2. No, horizontal screen mode is not supported.
  2. Can I mute the sound?
    3. The sound can be muted. "Music" and "Sound effects" can turned on or off under Home > OPTIONS.
How to operate the game
  1. I want to raise a new Tamagotchi.
    2. Select "Reset" under Home > OPTIONS. A new egg appears, which will eventually hatch into a Tamagotchi.
  2. I want to know when my Tamagotchi is calling me even when the app isn't running.
    3. Turn "Notifications" on under Home > OPTIONS.
  3. The screen is too dark to see anything.
    4. The light may be off. Use the light icon, and your Tamagotchi will appear.
Troubleshooting
  1. The app crashed suddenly.
    2. The main reason for the app crashing is a lack of available system memory. Free up some memory on your device, and launch the app again.
  2. My Tamagotchi is calling me too often. Is there anything I can do about this?
    3. Turn "Notifications" off under Home > OPTIONS.
  3. I accidently reset my Tamagotchi. Can I get it back?
    4. Once you perform a reset, there is no way to get your old Tamagotchi back.
  4. My Tamagotchi doesn't wake up or go to sleep!
    5. Your Tamagotchi's daily routine is based on your device settings. You can't raise them properly if the wrong time is set on your device. Also, avoid changing the clock on your device while raising Tamagotchis, as it can cause errors.
  5. My Tamagotchi died.
    6. This is likely because of one of the following:
    - It was hungry for too long.
    - It was sick for too long.
    - It got sick too many times.
    - It reached the end of its natural life.
    Press the Select button and Cancel button at the same time, or go to Home > OPTIONS and select the Reset button, and you can start the game again with a new egg.
